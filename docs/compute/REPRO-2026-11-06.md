# Reproduction manifest — every paper number, rebuilt from the artifacts

`benchmarks/repro_manifest.py` recomputes the AC-v2 numbers in
`papers/single-pass-decision-acv2.md` from the saved prediction JSONL, then compares them with the
quoted values. Pure CPU, seconds, no model.

```bash
python3 benchmarks/repro_manifest.py \
  --test backup-2026-10-29/s4090-1x-100/acwork/runs/E2-cur15k-V/eval-step938-V.jsonl \
  --val  backup-2026-10-29/Model_Training_Server/acwork/runs/E2-val/eval-E2final-V-val.jsonl
```

**Reading.** Seven of nine headline claims reproduce exactly from the artifact, including the one that
matters most for the paper's thesis (the type-routed gate AUROC 0.729). Two are flagged rather than
smoothed over:

1. **Pure-protocol numbers used to have no artifact; they do now.** The saved hinted run is 258/398. As of
   2026-11-07 the pure predictions exist in two places: **255/398** in-process
   (`backup-2026-10-29/.../E2-nomem/eval-E2final-nomem-V.jsonl`) and **254/398** through the served API
   (`backup-2026-11-06/offline/e2-pure-hist.jsonl`, every readout slot populated). Both are reported; neither is
   256, so the pre-audit 64.32 could not be reproduced.
2. **Certified coverage at 20% risk is estimator-dependent** (paper 40.7, this script 47.0). The paper
   used split-conformal with a val-fitted threshold; the script scans coverage fractions with a
   Clopper–Pearson upper bound on the val scores. Both are defensible; the disagreement is 6.3 pp,
   which is larger than the effect the paper claims is worth having, so the paper should quote the
   procedure explicitly or report the range.
