# Open issues — 2026-11-09

*The operator asked for the open issues to be written down and then researched, before any further training.
This file is the registry. Every issue below is either (a) a claim we make and cannot yet support, or (b) a
planned intervention with no evidence behind it. Each has an owner lane and a file it must produce.*

## The situation in one paragraph

We have a 1.92B single-pass decision model. On AndroidWorld-116 it scored **18.8% (15/80)** scorable —
measured, with a Wilson interval, against a published 2B bar of **45.1% SFT / 49.1% post-RL** (MAI-UI-2B).
The dominant failure is **not grounding and not calibration**: it is that the model is a one-click agent. On
the 80 scorable episodes, every episode where the model had to **type** burned the full 25-step budget and
failed (14 episodes, 1 success); every episode where it never typed finished in half the steps and succeeded
14 of 66 times. The proposed next move is training on multi-step trajectory data (GUI-Odyssey). The operator
asked the correct question: **is that actually going to work, and is the data valid?** Neither has been checked.

### The one real weakness the audit found

**7,638 of 9,005 episodes (84.8%) have non-contiguous step indices** — only 1,367 are contiguous 0..max. This is
the *subsampling* in `curate_ac.py`, not corruption: the compact set keeps a subset of each episode's steps. It
matters because (a) `history` is still the *full* teacher list, so a row's history may describe steps whose
screens are absent, and (b) any trajectory-order training built on these files must reconstruct order from
`step`, not from row adjacency.

**Duplicate gold labels on screen: 1,104 / 7,747 click rows (14.25%)** — two elements share the label
("Save", "Blank document", "Search here"). That is the real ambiguity rate in our subset, and it is a *scoring*
problem (a correct click on the sibling is marked wrong), not a labelling error. It is our own measured
equivalent of AndroidControl's "multiple valid" category, and it bounds any grounding claim: **at least 14% of
click rows have a defensible alternative target.**

### The 37.63% figure — provenance

We had been quoting "24.13% unclear task / 8.12% multiple valid / 37.63% wrong GT" second-hand. That is
AndroidControl-Curated's self-audit of a **different split**, and it is a *human semantic* judgement. A
structural audit cannot reproduce it and this audit does not claim to. What this audit can say is narrower and
solid: **our labels are internally consistent with the accessibility tree on 98.08% of rows, and free of
leakage, duplicates and status-placement errors.**

### What this audit cannot rule out

The human-annotator error: a click on the *wrong but plausible* widget is structurally perfect. To measure that
needs human adjudication on a sample. The sample size for ±4 pp at 95% is **601 rows** (worst case p=0.5);
for ±3 pp, **1,068**. That is the smallest defensible protocol, and it has not been run.

### Consequence for the training plan

**Do not spend effort re-cleaning the data.** The 1.92% flagged set is identifiable from
`flagged_rows.csv` and the 169 `no_scoreable_element_target` rows can simply be dropped. The data is not the
binding constraint; the *recipe* is (see A5). This retires the largest single risk in the issue list.

## Issue I2 — GUI-Odyssey usability: RESOLVED 2026-11-09 (my first reading was wrong)

**Status: measured against the real annotation file. The blocking objection is withdrawn.**

I first read the dataset card and concluded `TYPE` had no coordinates, so the corpus could not teach field
selection. **That was wrong — I read the card instead of the data.** Full measurement:
`docs/research/A1-gui-odyssey-usability.md`.

What the card gets wrong, and what the data actually says:

| Card claim | Measured in `all_anno.json` |
|---|---|
| action `TYPE` | action is **`TEXT`** (12,405 steps, 10.44%) |
| action `IMPOSSIBLE` | action is **`INCOMPLETE`** (249) |
| `HOME`, `BACK` exist | **0 occurrences each** |
| (card does not say) | `TEXT` is preceded by a `CLICK` on **12,314 / 12,405 = 99.27%** of rows |

**The decisive fact: the field *is* localised — by the click immediately before the typing.** So typing is
supervised in two-step form (the click carries the coordinate, `TEXT` carries the string). The corpus *can*
teach "which field do I type into"; the training example must be built as a (click, type) pair, not a single row.

Measured corpus: **7,735 episodes / 118,791 steps**, median 14 steps, 96.8% end in `COMPLETE`, **86.7% of
episodes contain text entry** (vs 19/104 in our AndroidWorld run) — that is exactly the imbalance I4/M1 names.

What it genuinely lacks: `open_app` (no launcher/home action), `navigate_back`, `wait`. Those are ~6% of our
cur15k; keep them from AndroidControl.

Remaining real concerns, both measured and new:

1. **One-third of screenshots are not portrait** — 1080x2400, 1440x3120, 1344x2992, 720x1280, 2208x1840,
   plus **2560x1600 landscape** (1,281 episodes). 67.3% portrait. Our model has never seen tablet/landscape.
2. **Episode count**: the file has 7,735; the paper says 8,334. Gap of 599 unexplained. Trust the file.
3. The newer release (`hflqf88888/GUIOdyssey`) adds `description`, `intention`, `low_level_instruction`,
   `context` and `sam2_bbox`. Checked a `TEXT` step there: **`sam2_bbox` is `[]`** — still not localised.
   But `low_level_instruction` + `context` give a real progress signal the old release lacked.

## Issue I3 — Trajectory SFT: RESEARCHED 2026-11-09. The answer is "inferred", and one of our own numbers was wrong

**Status: investigated in depth. See `docs/research/A2-trajectory-sft-evidence.md` (41 KB, 10 papers).**

**Verdict: no qualifying study exists.** No paper trains a ≤4B GUI model on multi-step trajectory data and
reports an end-to-end task-success delta *attributable to the trajectory data itself* against a single-step
control at the same base model, scale and compute. Every ≤4B end-to-end number in the literature is a
whole-pipeline bundle (trajectory + grounding + CoT + synthetic envs + RL). **At 2B specifically: zero papers.**
The plan therefore rests on an inference and must be labelled as one.

**Three findings that change what we do:**

1. **Our "92.3% / 93.1% per-step" figure for MAI-UI-2B does not exist.** MAI-UI publishes **no per-step metric
   at all** — Table 9 is task success only. Those numbers were *we* inverting `P(task)=p^n` with n=10 assumed,
   then cited as `[maiui2026, Tab. 9]`. Logged as mistake **M13** and retracted.
2. **`P(task)=p^n` is refuted by our own data.** It predicts 2.84% at 8 steps; we measured **18.8%** — a
   **6.6×** discrepancy. Step independence is false. Logged as **M14**; paper §5.12.1.1 now states this.
3. **The closest controlled evidence is Ferret-UI Lite-3B (Apple), which is end-to-end and decomposes.** Its
   ladder: 13.7 baseline → +2.1 short CoT → +3.8 long CoT → +0.7 first 5K synthetic traj → +2.1 → +2.8 (17K).
   **Synthetic trajectory data on top of a trajectory-SFT baseline is worth +5.6 points e2e (19.6 → 25.2)** —
   supportive. But the *first* 5K trajectories buy only **+0.7**, and the single biggest jump in the whole
   ladder is **long CoT (+3.8)**, a supervision-format change rather than a trajectory-data change.

**Consequence:** trajectory data is a real lever but it is **one of five ingredients and not the largest
measured one**. The plan must say so, and §6 of A5 carries the full ingredient checklist.

## Issue I4 — The typing failure: DIAGNOSED 2026-11-09 (and M2 is refuted)

**Status: RESOLVED. See `docs/research/A3-typing-and-termination.md`.**

**Verdict: the action slot works; the state model does not.** All **19/19** typing episodes emit a
`type_text` with a **non-empty string and a valid point, `action_ok=True`, `invalid_action=False`.** The field is
found and the string is right. What fails is everything after: **15 to 22 further clicks** on tasks needing 2–5
more actions.

| | n | successes | median steps |
|---|---|---|---|
| emitted `type_text` ≥1× | 19 | **1** | 25 (budget) |
| never | 106 | 15 | 7 |

Post-typing behaviour splits into **7 LOOP** (re-tapping one point: `ContactsNewContactDraft`, 15 clicks inside
a 14×217 px strip) and **5 SEARCH** (hunting the whole screen: `MarkorChangeNoteContent`, x 255–909,
y 208–1170). Both are "no memory of what I just did", not "aimed at the wrong button".

**M2 (readout-slot collapse) is REFUTED for `type_text`.** The pre-fix `app_name` collapse was real but is a
different slot and already repaired. Do not re-open it.

**M1 and M3 are the same defect.** 3.0% of labels are `type_text`; 22.0% are `status`; 17.6% of rows have empty
history. The corpus teaches "declare done" 7× more often than "type", and the model's behaviour mirrors that
prior. GUI-Odyssey corrects exactly this: **86.7% of its episodes contain text entry** and `TEXT` is 10.44% of
its steps.

Six post-processing remedies are already refuted (see A3 §3); **the fix has to be a supervision change.**
