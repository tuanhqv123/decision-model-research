our enhanced GRPO with data curriculum, repetition penalty, and experience replay achieves a +6.0

## Benchmark definition (verbatim)
"AndroidWorld (Rawles et al., 2024b) provides 116 tasks across 20 Android apps in a live Android emulator,
and requires continuous interaction with dynamic mobile environment."

## NOTE ON SCOPE
The AndroidWorld paper (arXiv:2405.14573v2) reports M3A at 30.6% (a11y tree, GPT-4 Turbo) and human at 80.0%.
The step count per task is NOT published as a single number; §5.12.1 uses n = 10 and states that choice.
